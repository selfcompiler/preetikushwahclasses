Answer Key & Detailed Solutions
Q1. [1 Mark]
(b) flows — Simple present tense. This is a geographical fact (universal truth). Third-person singular “The Ganga” takes “flows”.
Q2. [1 Mark]
ruled — Simple past tense. “Ancient India” tells us this happened in the past. Rule → ruled (regular verb).
Q3. [1 Mark]
(c) will launch — Simple future tense. “Next month” is a future time marker. Structure: will + V1.
Q4. [1 Mark]
did not attend — Simple past negative. “Last week” shows past. Structure: did not + V1.
Q5. [1 Mark]
taught — Teach is an irregular verb: teach (V1) → taught (V2) → taught (V3).
Q6. [2 Marks]
Simple Past: The shopkeeper sold fresh vegetables. (1 mark)
Simple Future: The shopkeeper will sell fresh vegetables. (1 mark)
Sell → sold (V2, irregular) / will sell (will + V1).
Q7. [2 Marks]
(a) She goes to the temple every Sunday. — Third-person singular “she” requires “goes” (V1 + es). (1 mark)
(b) They bought mangoes from the mandi yesterday. — “Buyed” is incorrect. Buy is irregular: buy → bought. (1 mark)
Q8. [2 Marks]
Negative: Amit did not score the highest marks in the class. (1 mark)
Interrogative: Did Amit score the highest marks in the class? (1 mark)
Use “did” + V1 (score) for both forms in simple past.
Q9. [2 Marks]
(a) bring — brought — brought (½ mark)
(b) catch — caught — caught (½ mark)
(c) fly — flew — flown (½ mark)
(d) write — wrote — written (½ mark)
Q10. [2 Marks]
(a) yesterday → Simple Past (½ mark)
(b) every day → Simple Present (½ mark)
(c) next week → Simple Future (½ mark)
(d) last Diwali → Simple Past (½ mark)
Q11. [3 Marks]
“Last year, our school organised an annual sports day. Students participated in various events. The principal gave away the prizes.”
organises → organised; participate → participated; gives → gave (irregular). (1 mark per correctly converted sentence)
Q12. [3 Marks]
(a) Add “-s”: Most verbs — He plays cricket. She reads books. (1 mark)
(b) Add “-es”: Verbs ending in s, sh, ch, x, o — He watches TV. She goes home. (1 mark)
(c) Change “y” to “-ies”: Verbs ending in consonant + y — She studies hard. He carries the bag. (1 mark)
Q13. [3 Marks]
(a) “The Rajdhani Express leaves at 5 PM” uses simple present for a fixed timetable or schedule. Trains, buses, flights follow set schedules, so we use simple present even for future events. (1 mark)
(b) “We will leave for Mumbai tomorrow” uses simple future for a personal plan or decision about the future. (1 mark)
Key rule: Simple present is used for official timetables, scheduled events, and universal truths even when referring to future time. (1 mark)
Q14. [3 Marks]
Sample paragraph:
“On 15th August, our school celebrated Independence Day with great enthusiasm. The principal hoisted the national flag. Students sang patriotic songs and performed a march-past. Teachers distributed sweets to everyone. We all felt proud to be Indian.”
(1 mark for correct past tense throughout, 1 mark for at least 4 underlined verbs, 1 mark for coherent paragraph)
Q15. [3 Marks]
(a) The Nehru family contributed greatly to India’s freedom struggle. — Simple Past Tense (historical fact). (1 mark)
(b) Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius. — Simple Present Tense (universal truth/scientific fact). (1 mark)
(c) Our class teacher will take us on a field trip next Friday. — Simple Future Tense (planned future action). (1 mark)
