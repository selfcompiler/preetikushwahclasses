Model Answers
Q1.
A formal letter ends with “Yours faithfully” (if name is unknown) or “Yours sincerely” (if name is known). An informal letter ends with “Yours lovingly”, “Your friend”, or “With love”.
Q2.
Three linking words: moreover, however, therefore. (Other correct answers: firstly, in addition, furthermore, also, finally, on the other hand, as a result.)
Q3.
We write formal letters to officials, authorities, or strangers. Examples: (1) The Principal of a school, (2) The Editor of a newspaper. (Also accepted: Municipal Commissioner, Bank Manager, etc.)
Q4.
A concluding sentence is the last sentence of a paragraph that summarises the main idea or gives a final thought. It wraps up the paragraph.
Q5.
The “Subject” line is written below the receiver’s address and above the salutation in a formal letter.
Q6.
Format of an Informal Letter:
1. Sender’s Address (top right/left)
2. Date
3. Salutation (Dear Riya, / Dear Uncle,)
4. Body (2–3 paragraphs)
5. Closing (Yours lovingly / Your friend / With love)
6. Name
Q7.
Topic sentence: “Reading books is one of the best habits a person can develop.”
Concluding sentence: “Therefore, we should all make time to read every day, as books truly are our best companions.”

Q9.
Three errors: (1) “dear” should be “Dear” (capital D), (2) “i” should be “I” (always capitalised), (3) Missing full stop after “area” (two sentences joined without punctuation).
Corrected: “Dear Sir, I am writing to you about the noise pollution in our area. I request you to take action.”
Q10.
Correct order: (b), (a), (e), (d), (c)
“The Swachh Bharat Mission aims to make India clean. It was started by Mahatma Gandhi. Today, the government continues this mission with great effort. Schools also organise cleanliness drives regularly. Every citizen must do their part to keep their surroundings clean.”
