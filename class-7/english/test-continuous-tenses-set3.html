Answer Key & Detailed Solutions
Q1. [1 Mark]
(b) believe — “Believe” is a stative verb (mental state). We say “I believe you”, NOT “I am believing you”.
Q2. [1 Mark]
Subject + was/were + verb-ing. “Was” is used with I/he/she/it; “were” is used with we/you/they.
Q3. [1 Mark]
Future Continuous Tense — “will be guarding” shows an action that will be in progress at a specific future time.
Q4. [1 Mark]
sitting — When a short verb ends in consonant-vowel-consonant (CVC), double the last consonant before adding -ing: sit → sitting.
Q5. [1 Mark]
False — “Understand” is a stative verb (mental state). Correct: “I understand the lesson.”
Q6. [1 Mark]
Any two of: now, right now, at this moment, at present, currently, look!, listen!
Q7. [2 Marks]
Past Continuous: The mechanic was repairing our car. (1 mark)
Future Continuous: The mechanic will be repairing our car. (1 mark)
Q8. [2 Marks]
Neha was cooking dinner when the electricity went off. (1 mark for correct combination, 1 mark for correct tenses)
The ongoing action (cooking) is in past continuous; the interrupting action (went off) is in simple past.
Q9. [2 Marks]
(a) The boys are running in the playground. — Plural subject “boys” needs “are”, not “is”. (1 mark)
(b) She was dancing gracefully on stage. — Singular subject “she” needs “was”, not “were”. (1 mark)
Q10. [2 Marks]
(a) die → dying (change “ie” to “y” + ing) (½ mark)
(b) swim → swimming (CVC rule: double the last consonant) (½ mark)
(c) hope → hoping (drop silent “e” + ing) (½ mark)
(d) travel → travelling (double “l” in British English) (½ mark)
Q11. [2 Marks]
(a) Action happening now: “Mummy is cooking rajma for lunch right now.” — describes what is in progress at this moment. (1 mark)
(b) Planned future action: “We are visiting the Taj Mahal next Sunday.” — present continuous can express a fixed plan. (1 mark)
Q12. [3 Marks]
“The children were playing in the park. Birds were singing on the trees. A vendor was selling ice cream near the gate.”
(1 mark for each correctly converted sentence)
Q13. [3 Marks]
(a) “The train stops at every station” = habitual/general fact (simple present). “The train is stopping at Platform 3” = happening right now (present continuous). (1 mark)
(b) “She reads novels” = general hobby. “She is reading a novel” = specific action in progress. (1 mark)
(c) “He plays cricket” = regular activity. “He is playing cricket” = playing right now. (1 mark)
Q14. [3 Marks]
Sample paragraph:
“Last year, we were decorating our classroom with diyas and rangoli for Diwali. (Past Continuous) Our teacher was telling us stories about the festival. (Past Continuous) Right now, our school is preparing for the Diwali celebration. (Present Continuous) Students are making greeting cards. (Present Continuous) Tomorrow, we will be performing a cultural programme on stage. (Future Continuous) Everyone will be enjoying sweets and crackers.” (Future Continuous)
(1 mark for each tense type used correctly)
Q15. [3 Marks]
Stative verbs express states, not actions. They describe emotions, mental states, or possession.
1. belong — “This book belongs to me.” (NOT “is belonging”) — expresses possession. (1 mark)
2. prefer — “I prefer tea over coffee.” (NOT “am preferring”) — expresses a mental state. (1 mark)
3. love — “She loves her grandmother.” (NOT “is loving”) — expresses an emotion. (1 mark)
Q16. [5 Marks]
(a) Continuous tense verbs: was studying, were playing, was preparing, was winning, is practising, will be representing (1 mark)
(b) was studying — Past Continuous; were playing — Past Continuous; was preparing — Past Continuous; was winning — Past Continuous; is practising — Present Continuous; will be representing — Future Continuous (1 mark)
(c) “Heard” is a momentary/sudden action — it happened instantly and was not ongoing, so simple past is used. (1 mark)
(d) Arjun is not practising daily because next month he will not be representing his school in the district tournament. (1 mark)
(e) Was Arjun studying for his exams last Sunday? OR Were his friends playing cricket outside? (1 mark)
Q17. [5 Marks]
(a) Affirmative: (2 marks)
Present: I am writing / He is writing / We are writing / They are writing
Past: I was writing / He was writing / We were writing / They were writing
Future: I will be writing / He will be writing / We will be writing / They will be writing

(b) Negative: (2 marks)
Present: I am not writing / He is not writing / We are not writing / They are not writing
Past: I was not writing / He was not writing / We were not writing / They were not writing
Future: I will not be writing / He will not be writing / We will not be writing / They will not be writing

(c) Interrogative: (1 mark)
Present: Am I writing? / Is he writing?
Past: Was I writing? / Was he writing?
Future: Will I be writing? / Will he be writing?
Q18. Bonus [2 Marks]
“Have” as possession (stative): “I have a bicycle.” (NOT “I am having a bicycle”) — here “have” means ownership, which is a state. (1 mark)
“Have” as an action: “I am having breakfast.” — here “have” means eating, which is an action, so continuous tense IS allowed. (1 mark)
