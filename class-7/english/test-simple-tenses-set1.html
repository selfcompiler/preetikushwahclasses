Answer Key & Detailed Solutions
Q1. [1 Mark]
goes — Simple present tense. “Every morning” shows a habitual action. Third-person singular (father) takes “goes” (verb + es).
Q2. [1 Mark]
visited — Simple past tense. “Last summer” indicates a completed action in the past. Visit → visited (regular verb).
Q3. [1 Mark]
will celebrate / shall celebrate — Simple future tense. “Next Monday” signals a future action. Structure: will/shall + V1.
Q4. [1 Mark]
rises — Simple present tense. This is a universal truth/scientific fact. Third-person singular takes “rises”.
Q5. [1 Mark]
told — Simple past tense. “Yesterday night” shows a completed past action. Tell → told (irregular verb).
Q6. [2 Marks]
(a) Simple Present Tense — verb: drinks. “Every night” shows a habitual action. (1 mark)
(b) Simple Past Tense — verb: played. The action is completed in the past. (1 mark)
Q7. [2 Marks]
(a) Simple Future Tense — verb: will join. “Next year” indicates a future action. (1 mark)
(b) Simple Past Tense — verb: sang. The action happened in the past (at the assembly). (1 mark)
Q8. [2 Marks]
(a) Simple Present Tense — verb: arrives. “Daily” shows a routine/timetable event. (1 mark)
(b) Simple Future Tense — verb: shall plant. A planned future action. (1 mark)
Q9. [2 Marks]
(a) Simple Past Tense — verb: bought. Buy → bought (irregular). Completed action. (1 mark)
(b) Simple Present Tense — verb: cooks. Habitual action (she does this regularly). (1 mark)
Q10. [2 Marks]
(a) Simple Future Tense — verb: will travel. “During the holidays” is a future time. (1 mark)
(b) Simple Past Tense — verb: completed. “Before dinner” in past context. (1 mark)
Q11. [2 Marks]
The farmer ploughed his field in the morning.
Change verb from V1 (ploughs) to V2 (ploughed). Remove the “s” and use past form. (1 mark for correct verb, 1 mark for correct sentence)
Q12. [2 Marks]
Kavita will write a letter to her friend.
Change verb from V2 (wrote) to will + V1 (will write). (1 mark for correct helping verb, 1 mark for correct base form)
Q13. [2 Marks]
The postman delivers letters to our house.
Change verb from V2 (delivered) to V1 with “s” (delivers) since postman is third-person singular. (1 mark for correct verb form, 1 mark for correct sentence)
Q14. [2 Marks]
Ravi does not go to school by bus.
In simple present negative, add “does not” before the base form of the verb. “Goes” becomes “go”. (1 mark for “does not”, 1 mark for base form “go”)
Q15. [2 Marks]
Did Priya finish her painting yesterday?
In simple past interrogative, use “Did” at the beginning and change V2 to V1. “Finished” becomes “finish”. Add a question mark. (1 mark for correct word order, 1 mark for base form + question mark)
