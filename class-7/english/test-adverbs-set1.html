Class VII English Grammar — Foundation Level

Preeti Kushwah Classes

Total Marks: 25 Time: 40 min
General Instructions:
1. All questions are compulsory.
2. Section A: 5 questions of 1 mark each.
3. Section B: 5 questions of 2 marks each.
4. Section C: 5 questions of 2 marks each.
5. Write neat and clear answers.
Section A — Pick the Adverb (1 Mark Each) [5 × 1 = 5]
Q1.1
Identify the adverb in the sentence: Rahul quickly finished his homework before the cricket match.
Q2.1
Identify the adverb in the sentence: The train will arrive here at 6 o’clock.
Q3.1
Identify the adverb in the sentence: My grandmother always tells us stories at bedtime.
Q4.1
Identify the adverb in the sentence: The kite flew very high in the sky during Makar Sankranti.
Q5.1
Identify the adverb in the sentence: She sang the bhajan beautifully at the temple.
Section B — Type of Adverb (2 Marks Each) [5 × 2 = 10]
Q6.2
Identify the adverb and state its type:
The students waited patiently for the school bus.
Q7.2
Identify the adverb and state its type:
We visited the Taj Mahal yesterday.
Q8.2
Identify the adverb and state its type:
The children played everywhere in the park.
Q9.2
Identify the adverb and state its type:
Priya has almost completed her Rangoli design.
Q10.2
Identify the adverb and state its type:
He rarely misses his morning yoga practice.
Section C — Fill in the Blanks with Suitable Adverbs (2 Marks Each) [5 × 2 = 10]
Q11.2
Fill in the blanks with suitable adverbs:
(a) The mango fell ___ from the tree. (adverb of manner)
(b) We will go to Jaipur ___. (adverb of time)
Q12.2
Form adverbs from the given adjectives:
(a) careful → ___
(b) happy → ___
Q13.2
Rewrite the sentence by placing the adverb correctly:
She dances. (gracefully)
Also state the type of adverb used.
Q14.2
Fill in the blanks with suitable adverbs:
(a) Aman ___ goes to the library on Sundays. (adverb of frequency)
(b) The dog was barking ___. (adverb of manner)
Q15.2
Identify the adverbs and state what each one modifies (verb, adjective, or another adverb):
The extremely talented dancer performed very gracefully.

Answer Key & Solutions
Q1. [1 Mark]
quickly — It tells how Rahul finished his homework (modifies the verb ‘finished’).
Q2. [1 Mark]
here — It tells where the train will arrive (adverb of place).
Q3. [1 Mark]
always — It tells how often grandmother tells stories (adverb of frequency).
Q4. [1 Mark]
very and high — ‘high’ tells how far the kite flew; ‘very’ modifies ‘high’. Accept either.
Q5. [1 Mark]
beautifully — It tells how she sang (adverb of manner).
Q6. [2 Marks]
Adverb: patiently [1 mark]
Type: Adverb of manner — It describes how the students waited. [1 mark]
Q7. [2 Marks]
Adverb: yesterday [1 mark]
Type: Adverb of time — It tells when we visited the Taj Mahal. [1 mark]
Q8. [2 Marks]
Adverb: everywhere [1 mark]
Type: Adverb of place — It tells where the children played. [1 mark]
Q9. [2 Marks]
Adverb: almost [1 mark]
Type: Adverb of degree — It tells to what extent the work is completed. [1 mark]
Q10. [2 Marks]
Adverb: rarely [1 mark]
Type: Adverb of frequency — It tells how often he misses yoga. [1 mark]
Q11. [2 Marks]
(a) The mango fell down / softly from the tree. [1 mark]
(b) We will go to Jaipur tomorrow / soon. [1 mark]
(Accept any correct adverb of the given type.)
Q12. [2 Marks]
(a) careful → carefully (add -ly) [1 mark]
(b) happy → happily (change y to i, add -ly) [1 mark]
Q13. [2 Marks]
She dances gracefully. [1 mark]
Type: Adverb of manner — It tells how she dances. [1 mark]
Q14. [2 Marks]
(a) Aman often / usually / sometimes goes to the library on Sundays. [1 mark]
(b) The dog was barking loudly / fiercely. [1 mark]
Q15. [2 Marks]
extremely — modifies the adjective ‘talented’ (adverb of degree) [½ + ½]
very — modifies the adverb ‘gracefully’ (adverb of degree) [½ + ½]
gracefully — modifies the verb ‘performed’ (adverb of manner)
