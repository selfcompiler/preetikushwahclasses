General Instructions:
1. All questions are compulsory.
2. Section A: 5 × 1 = 5 | Section B: 5 × 2 = 10 | Section C: 5 × 2 = 10

Section A — MCQ / Fill in the Blank (1 Mark Each) [5 × 1 = 5]
Q1.1
Choose the correct pronoun: Rina and ___ are going to the temple.
(a) me   (b) I   (c) myself   (d) mine
Q2.1
Identify the pronoun in the sentence: Somebody left their tiffin box in the classroom.
Q3.1
Fill in with the correct pronoun: The book is on the table. Please pick ___ up.
Q4.1
Which type of pronoun is ‘who’ in the sentence: The girl who won the race is from Jaipur.
(a) personal   (b) reflexive   (c) relative   (d) interrogative
Q5.1
True or False: ‘Herself’ is a demonstrative pronoun.
Section B — Short Answer (2 Marks Each) [5 × 2 = 10]
Q6.2
Replace the underlined nouns with suitable pronouns:
(a) Ravi gave Ravi’s notebook to the teacher.
(b) The mangoes were sweet. The children enjoyed the mangoes.
Q7.2
Fill in with reflexive pronouns:
(a) Meera dressed ___ for the Diwali party.
(b) We enjoyed ___ at the school picnic in Nainital.
Q8.2
Identify the pronouns and state their type:
(a) This is the pen that my grandfather gave me.
(b) Who is knocking at the door?
Q9.2
Correct the errors in pronoun usage:
(a) Him and me went to the cricket match.
(b) Each of the girls forgot their umbrella.
Q10.2
What is a possessive pronoun? Give two examples in sentences.
Section C — Apply & Write (2 Marks Each) [5 × 2 = 10]
Q11.2
Fill in with the correct pronouns:
Ankit told ___ mother that ___ would finish ___ homework before dinner.
Q12.2
Use the correct form of the pronoun in brackets:
(a) This bag belongs to Sita. It is ___. (she)
(b) The children hurt ___ while playing kabaddi. (they)
Q13.2
Rewrite using pronouns in place of the repeated nouns:
Priya is a good student. Priya helps Priya’s classmates. Priya’s classmates like Priya very much.
Q14.2
Frame questions using interrogative pronouns for these answers:
(a) Answer: Rahul broke the window.
(b) Answer: She chose the red dupatta.
Q15.2
Write two sentences about your best friend — one using a personal pronoun and one using a possessive pronoun.

Answer Key & Solutions
Q1. [1 Mark]
(b) I — ‘I’ is a subject pronoun. When used with another noun as a compound subject (Rina and I), the subject form is required.
Q2. [1 Mark]
Somebody (indefinite pronoun) and their (possessive pronoun). Either answer accepted for 1 mark.
Q3. [1 Mark]
it — ‘It’ replaces the noun ‘book’ (a thing, singular, neuter gender).
Q4. [1 Mark]
(c) relative — ‘Who’ connects the clause ‘who won the race’ to the noun ‘girl’, making it a relative pronoun.
Q5. [1 Mark]
False — ‘Herself’ is a reflexive pronoun, not a demonstrative pronoun. Demonstrative pronouns are this, that, these, those.
Q6. [2 Marks]
(a) Ravi gave his notebook to the teacher. (‘Ravi’s’ replaced by possessive pronoun ‘his’.) [1 mark]
(b) They were sweet. The children enjoyed them. (‘The mangoes’ replaced by ‘They’ as subject and ‘them’ as object.) [1 mark]
Q7. [2 Marks]
(a) Meera dressed herself for the Diwali party. (Third person, singular, feminine.) [1 mark]
(b) We enjoyed ourselves at the school picnic in Nainital. (First person, plural.) [1 mark]
Q8. [2 Marks]
(a) This — demonstrative pronoun; that — relative pronoun; me — personal pronoun (object form). [1 mark]
(b) Who — interrogative pronoun (used to ask a question). [1 mark]
Q9. [2 Marks]
(a) He and I went to the cricket match. (Subject pronouns needed: ‘He’ not ‘Him’; ‘I’ not ‘me’.) [1 mark]
(b) Each of the girls forgot her umbrella. (‘Each’ is singular, so the pronoun must be singular: ‘her’.) [1 mark]
Q10. [2 Marks]
A possessive pronoun shows ownership and stands alone without a noun after it. [1 mark]
Examples: This bicycle is mine. / That house is theirs. [1 mark]
(Possessive pronouns: mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs.)
Q11. [2 Marks]
Ankit told his mother that he would finish his homework before dinner. [2 marks for all three correct; 1 mark for two correct]
Q12. [2 Marks]
(a) This bag belongs to Sita. It is hers. (Possessive pronoun, stands alone.) [1 mark]
(b) The children hurt themselves while playing kabaddi. (Reflexive pronoun, third person plural.) [1 mark]
Q13. [2 Marks]
Priya is a good student. She helps her classmates. They like her very much. [1 mark for correct subject/object replacements; 1 mark for possessive replacement]
Q14. [2 Marks]
(a) Who broke the window? [1 mark]
(b) Which dupatta did she choose? (or What did she choose?) [1 mark]
Q15. [2 Marks]
Sample:
Personal pronoun: She is my best friend and she always helps me. [1 mark]
Possessive pronoun: The blue pencil box is hers. [1 mark]
(Accept any correct sentences using the required pronoun types.)
