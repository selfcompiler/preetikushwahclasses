Answer Key & Detailed Solutions
Q1. [1 Mark]
Simple Present Tense — Verb form: V5 (plays).
“Plays” is the third-person singular present form of “play.” V5 = V1 + s/es. The clue word “every evening” indicates a habitual action (Simple Present).
Q2. [1 Mark]
Simple Past Tense — Verb form: V2 (went).
“Went” is the past form (V2) of “go.” The clue word “yesterday” confirms a completed past action.
Q3. [1 Mark]
Present Continuous Tense — Verb form: V4 (reading).
“Reading” is the present participle (V4 = V1 + ing). Formula: Subject + is/am/are + V4. Clue: “right now.”
Q4. [1 Mark]
Present Perfect Tense — Verb form: V3 (finished).
“Finished” is the past participle (V3) of “finish.” Formula: Subject + has/have + V3. Clue: “already.”
Q5. [1 Mark]
Simple Present Tense — Verb form: V1 (fly).
“Fly” is the base form (V1). With plural subjects (“birds”), we use V1 (not V5). The sentence states a general fact.
Q6. [2 Marks]
He ate an apple for breakfast yesterday.
Formula: Subject + V2. “Ate” is the V2 (past form) of “eat.” (V1: eat, V2: ate, V3: eaten). (1 mark for correct answer, 1 mark for identifying V2)
Q7. [2 Marks]
The children are playing in the garden right now.
Formula: Subject + is/am/are + V4. “Playing” is V4 (present participle = V1 + ing). Plural subject “children” takes “are.” (1 mark for “are playing,” 1 mark for identifying V4)
Q8. [2 Marks]
TenseFormulaVerb Form Used
Simple Present (3rd person singular)Subject + V5V5 (V1 + s/es)
Simple PastSubject + V2V2 (past form)
(1 mark for each correct row)
Q9. [2 Marks]
Sita wrote a letter to her friend.
V-form change: V5 → V2. “Writes” (V5 = write + s) changes to “wrote” (V2 = past form of write).
(1 mark for correct sentence, 1 mark for identifying V-form change)
Q10. [2 Marks]
I have spoken to the principal about the trip.
Formula: Subject + has/have + V3. “Spoken” is V3 (past participle) of “speak.” (V1: speak, V2: spoke, V3: spoken). Subject “I” takes “have.”
(1 mark for correct answer, 1 mark for identifying V3)
Q11. [2 Marks]
(a) Simple Future Tense: Subject + will/shall + V1 (base form).
Example: She will go (V1) to school tomorrow.
(b) Past Continuous Tense: Subject + was/were + V4 (present participle / V1 + ing).
Example: They were playing (V4) in the park.
(1 mark for each correct formula)
Q12. [2 Marks]
(a) Simple Past: She wrote a beautiful poem. — Verb form: V2 (wrote).
(b) Present Perfect: She has written a beautiful poem. — Verb form: V3 (written), with helping verb “has.”
(1 mark for each correct sentence with V-form identification)
Q13. [2 Marks]
Present Perfect uses V3 (past participle) with has/have:
Example: I have eaten (V3) my lunch. (has/have + V3)
Simple Past uses V2 (past form) alone:
Example: I ate (V2) my lunch.
Difference: Simple Past uses V2 without any helping verb, while Present Perfect uses V3 with has/have. V2 shows a completed past action; V3 with has/have connects the past action to the present.
(1 mark for correct V-form identification, 1 mark for clear explanation with examples)
Q14. [2 Marks]
(a) go: V1 = go, V2 = went, V3 = gone, V4 = going, V5 = goes
(b) sing: V1 = sing, V2 = sang, V3 = sung, V4 = singing, V5 = sings
(1 mark for each verb — all five forms must be correct for full marks)
Q15. [2 Marks]
(a) Present Continuous: The girls are dancing on the stage. — Verb form: V4 (dancing = V1 + ing).
(b) Simple Future: The girls will dance on the stage tomorrow. — Verb form: V1 (dance = base form, used with will/shall).
(1 mark for each correct sentence with V-form identification)
