Answer Key & Detailed Solutions
Section A — Fill in the Blanks
Q1. [1 Mark]
She is reading a storybook right now.
Present Continuous Tense — Formula: Subject + is/am/are + V4 (present participle).
“Read” → V4 = reading (V1 + ing). The helping verb “is” is used because the subject “She” is third-person singular. Signal word: “right now” shows an action happening at this moment.
Q2. [1 Mark]
They have been playing cricket since morning.
Present Perfect Continuous Tense — Formula: Subject + has/have + been + V4.
“Play” → V4 = playing (V1 + ing). “Have” is used because the subject “They” is plural. Signal word: “since morning” shows an action that started in the past and is still continuing.
Q3. [1 Mark]
Ravi had finished his homework before dinner yesterday.
Past Perfect Tense — Formula: Subject + had + V3 (past participle).
“Finish” → V3 = finished. Signal words: “before dinner yesterday” — the Past Perfect shows an action completed before another past action.
Q4. [1 Mark]
We will visit / shall visit Jaipur next summer.
Simple Future Tense — Formula: Subject + will/shall + V1 (base form).
“Visit” → V1 = visit (base form, no change). Signal word: “next summer” points to a future action.
Q5. [1 Mark]
By 8 o’clock tomorrow, she will have completed the painting.
Future Perfect Tense — Formula: Subject + will + have + V3 (past participle).
“Complete” → V3 = completed. Signal words: “By 8 o’clock tomorrow” — the action will be finished before a specific future time.
Section B — Identify the Tense
Q6. [1 Mark]
Simple Present Tense
Verb used: “cooks” = V5 (V1 + s). Formula: Subject + V5 (for third-person singular) or V1 (for others).
Signal word: “every day” shows a habitual/regular action. “Mother” is third-person singular, so V5 (cooks) is used instead of V1 (cook).
Q7. [1 Mark]
Past Continuous Tense
Verb used: “were writing” = was/were + V4 (writing). Formula: Subject + was/were + V4.
Signal: “when the bell rang” — shows an action that was going on in the past when another action interrupted it. “Students” is plural, so “were” is used.
Q8. [1 Mark]
Present Perfect Tense
Verb used: “has eaten” = has/have + V3 (eaten). Formula: Subject + has/have + V3.
Signal word: “already” — shows an action completed in the recent past with a connection to the present. V3 of “eat” = eaten.
Q9. [1 Mark]
Future Continuous Tense
Verb used: “shall be travelling” = will/shall + be + V4 (travelling). Formula: Subject + will/shall + be + V4.
Signal: “this time tomorrow” — shows an action that will be happening at a specific time in the future.
Q10. [1 Mark]
Past Perfect Continuous Tense
Verb used: “had been practising” = had + been + V4 (practising). Formula: Subject + had + been + V4.
Signal words: “for two months before it started” — shows an action that was continuing for a period of time before another past action happened.
Section C — Match the Following
Q11. [5 Marks — 1 mark for each correct match]
(a) → (ii) Simple Past Tense — “won” is V2 of “win.” Signal: “yesterday” = completed past action.
(b) → (v) Present Perfect Tense — “has lived” = has + V3. Signal: “since 2018” = past action connected to present.
(c) → (i) Future Perfect Continuous Tense — “will have been building” = will + have + been + V4. Signal: “By next July” + “for three years” = action continuing up to a future point.
(d) → (iii) Simple Present Tense — “rises” is V5. This is a universal truth/fact, which always uses Simple Present.
(e) → (iv) Past Continuous Tense — “were playing” = were + V4. Signal: “when it started raining” = ongoing past action interrupted by another event.
Section D — Correct the Errors
Q12. [1 Mark]
Correct: She has gone to school already.
Error: “have” was used with “She” (third-person singular). The correct helping verb is “has” (not “have”).
Tense: Present Perfect — Subject + has/have + V3. V3 of “go” = gone. With She/He/It, always use “has.”
Q13. [1 Mark]
Correct: We were watching television when the power went off.
Error: “was” was used with “We” (plural subject). The correct helping verb is “were” (not “was”).
Tense: Past Continuous — Subject + was/were + V4. Use “was” with I/He/She/It and “were” with We/You/They.
Q14. [1 Mark]
Correct: He has written three letters to his friend.
Error: “wrote” (V2) was used instead of “written” (V3). Present Perfect needs V3, not V2.
Tense: Present Perfect — Subject + has/have + V3. V1: write, V2: wrote, V3: written.
Q15. [1 Mark]
Correct: They will be waiting for us at the station tomorrow.
Error: “will been waiting” is incorrect. The correct structure is “will be + V4” (not “will been”).
Tense: Future Continuous — Subject + will/shall + be + V4. V4 of “wait” = waiting.
Q16. [1 Mark]
Correct: Rohan plays cricket every Sunday.
Error: “play” (V1) was used with “Rohan” (third-person singular). For third-person singular subjects in Simple Present, we use V5 (V1 + s/es).
Tense: Simple Present — Subject + V5 (for He/She/It/Name) or V1 (for I/We/You/They). V5 of “play” = plays.
Section E — Make Sentences
Q17. [1 Mark]
Present Perfect Continuous Tense — Formula: Subject + has/have + been + V4.
Sample Answer: The children have been playing cricket since 4 o’clock.
V4 of “play” = playing (V1 + ing). Any grammatically correct sentence using has/have + been + playing will receive full marks.
Q18. [1 Mark]
Simple Past Tense — Formula: Subject + V2.
Sample Answer: Meera wrote a letter to her grandmother yesterday.
V2 of “write” = wrote. Any grammatically correct sentence using wrote (V2) will receive full marks.
Q19. [1 Mark]
Future Continuous Tense — Formula: Subject + will/shall + be + V4.
Sample Answer: I shall be studying for exams this time tomorrow.
V4 of “study” = studying (V1 + ing). Any grammatically correct sentence using will/shall + be + studying will receive full marks.
Q20. [1 Mark]
Past Perfect Tense — Formula: Subject + had + V3.
Sample Answer: The choir had sung the national anthem before the match began.
V3 of “sing” = sung (V1: sing, V2: sang, V3: sung). Any grammatically correct sentence using had + sung will receive full marks.
Q21. [1 Mark]
Present Perfect Tense — Formula: Subject + has/have + V3.
Sample Answer: I have eaten my breakfast already.
V3 of “eat” = eaten (V1: eat, V2: ate, V3: eaten). Any grammatically correct sentence using has/have + eaten will receive full marks.
