THE SENTENCE — TEST PAPER SET 1

Class VII English Grammar — Foundation Level

Preeti Kushwah Classes

Total Marks: 25 Time: 45 min
General Instructions:
1. All questions are compulsory.
2. Section A: 5 questions of 1 mark each.
3. Section B: 5 questions of 2 marks each.
4. Section C: 5 questions of 2 marks each.
5. Write neat and clear answers.
Section A — Identify the Sentence Type (1 Mark Each) [5 × 1 = 5]
Q1.1
Identify the type of sentence: The Republic Day parade takes place on 26th January every year.
Q2.1
Identify the type of sentence: Have you ever visited the Qutub Minar?
Q3.1
Identify the type of sentence: Please submit your homework by tomorrow morning.
Q4.1
Identify the type of sentence: What a magnificent fort Mehrangarh is!
Q5.1
Identify the type of sentence: Do not pluck the flowers from the garden.
Section B — Subject and Predicate (2 Marks Each) [5 × 2 = 10]
Q6.2
Separate the subject and predicate:
The farmers of Punjab harvest wheat in April.
Q7.2
Separate the subject and predicate:
My grandmother’s delicious mango pickle is famous in our colony.
Q8.2
Separate the subject and predicate:
The school bus arrives at the gate every morning at eight o’clock.
Q9.2
Separate the subject and predicate:
A group of monkeys was sitting on the temple wall.
Q10.2
Separate the subject and predicate:
The tall coconut trees swayed gently in the sea breeze.
Section C — Rearrange & Punctuate (2 Marks Each) [5 × 2 = 10]
Q11.2
Rearrange the words to form a meaningful sentence:
river / the / Ganga / through / flows / Varanasi
Q12.2
Rearrange the words to form a meaningful sentence:
cricket / plays / every / Rohit / evening / in / the / park
Q13.2
Add correct punctuation and rewrite:
what time does the rajdhani express leave from new delhi station
Q14.2
State whether the following group of words is a sentence or not. Give a reason:
Under the old banyan tree near the village pond.
Q15.2
Convert the following assertive sentence into an interrogative sentence:
Diwali is the festival of lights.

Answer Key & Solutions
Q1. [1 Mark]
Assertive / Declarative sentence — It states a fact about the Republic Day parade.
Q2. [1 Mark]
Interrogative sentence — It asks a question (ends with a question mark).
Q3. [1 Mark]
Imperative sentence — It makes a polite request (begins with ‘Please’).
Q4. [1 Mark]
Exclamatory sentence — It expresses a strong feeling of admiration (begins with ‘What’ and ends with ‘!’).
Q5. [1 Mark]
Imperative sentence — It gives a command/prohibition (‘Do not’).
Q6. [2 Marks]
Subject: The farmers of Punjab [1 mark]
Predicate: harvest wheat in April [1 mark]
Q7. [2 Marks]
Subject: My grandmother’s delicious mango pickle [1 mark]
Predicate: is famous in our colony [1 mark]
Q8. [2 Marks]
Subject: The school bus [1 mark]
Predicate: arrives at the gate every morning at eight o’clock [1 mark]
Q9. [2 Marks]
Subject: A group of monkeys [1 mark]
Predicate: was sitting on the temple wall [1 mark]
Q10. [2 Marks]
Subject: The tall coconut trees [1 mark]
Predicate: swayed gently in the sea breeze [1 mark]
Q11. [2 Marks]
The river Ganga flows through Varanasi. [1 mark for correct order, 1 mark for punctuation]
Q12. [2 Marks]
Rohit plays cricket every evening in the park. [1 mark for correct order, 1 mark for punctuation]
Q13. [2 Marks]
What time does the Rajdhani Express leave from New Delhi station? [1 mark for capitals, 1 mark for question mark]
Q14. [2 Marks]
Not a sentence. [1 mark]
Reason: It has no verb and no predicate; it is just a phrase that does not express a complete thought. [1 mark]
Q15. [2 Marks]
Is Diwali the festival of lights? [1 mark for correct conversion, 1 mark for question mark]
Also accept: Isn’t Diwali the festival of lights?
