Answer Key & Detailed Solutions
Q1. [1 Mark]
9/10 = 0.9
(Denominator is 10 → 1 decimal place)
Q2. [1 Mark]
6.83: tenths place = 8, hundredths place = 3
Answer: 3
Q3. [1 Mark]
They are equal. 0.5 = 0.50
A trailing zero after the last non-zero digit in the decimal part does not change the value.
Neither is greater — 0.5 = 0.50.
Q4. [1 Mark]
3.4 + 2.5 = 5.9
Q5. [1 Mark]
0.75 = 75/100
HCF of 75 and 100 = 25
75/100 = 3/4
Q6. [1 Mark]
“Forty-five hundredths” = 45/100 = 0.45
Q7. [2 Marks]
2/5 — we need denominator = 10.
Multiply numerator and denominator by 2:
2/5 = (2×2)/(5×2) = 4/10 = 0.4
Q8. [2 Marks]
Convert to like decimals (2 decimal places): 3.20, 3.02, 3.20, 3.21
Compare hundredths: 3.02 (02) < 3.20 (20) = 3.20 (20) < 3.21 (21)
Ascending order: 3.02 < 3.2 = 3.20 < 3.21

Q11. [2 Marks]
(a) 5.3 × 10 = 53 (shift decimal 1 place right)
(b) 5.3 × 100 = 530 (shift decimal 2 places right)
Rule: Multiplying a decimal by 10 shifts the decimal point one place to the right. By 100 shifts it two places right.

Q13. [3 Marks]
(a) 67.04 = 60 + 7 + 0.04  (or: 60 + 7 + 4/100)

(b) 50 + 3 + 7/10 + 2/100 = 53 + 0.7 + 0.02 = 53.72

(c) 0.7 ÷ 0.07 = 7/10 ÷ 7/100 = 7/10 × 100/7 = 10
So 0.7 is 10 times greater than 0.07.
Q14. [3 Marks]
(a) Expression: 12.5 − 3.75 − 4.25

(b) 12.50 − 3.75 = 8.75;   8.75 − 4.25 = 4.50 m

(c) Cloth remaining = 4.50 m; cloth needed for another shirt = 3.75 m
4.50 ≥ 3.75, so Yes, she has enough cloth. She will have 4.50 − 3.75 = 0.75 m left over.
Q15. [3 Marks]
(a) 2.35 + 1.80 + 0.95 = 2.35 + 1.80 = 4.15;  4.15 + 0.95 = 5.10 m

(b) Longest = 2.35 m, Shortest = 0.95 m
2.35 − 0.95 = 1.40 m

(c) Total length = 5.10 m > 5 m, so Yes, they will reach the wall.
They exceed by 5.10 − 5.00 = 0.10 m.
Q16. [5 Marks]
(a) Rice: 2.5 × 40 = ₹100.00
(b) Sugar: 1.75 × 48 = 1.75 × 48 = (1 × 48) + (0.75 × 48) = 48 + 36 = ₹84.00
(c) Oil: 0.5 × 120 = ₹60.00
(d) Total = 100 + 84 + 60 = ₹244.00
(e) Balance = 400 − 244 = ₹156.00
Q17. [5 Marks]
(a) Convert to like decimals (3 places): 4.500, 4.050, 4.550, 4.505
Compare: 4.550 > 4.505 > 4.500 > 4.050
Descending: 4.55 > 4.505 > 4.5 > 4.05

(b) Largest = 4.55, Smallest = 4.05
Sum = 4.55 + 4.05 = 8.60

(c) Difference = 4.55 − 4.05 = 0.50

(d) Smallest (4.05) × 100 = 405
Q18. [Bonus — 2 Marks]
Let hundredths digit = d.
Then tenths digit = d + 3.
Whole part = 7.
Sum of digits: 7 + (d + 3) + d = 12
⇒ 10 + 2d = 12 ⇒ 2d = 2 ⇒ d = 1
Tenths digit = 1 + 3 = 4, Hundredths digit = 1
The decimal number is 7.41
Verification: 7 + 4 + 1 = 12 ✓
