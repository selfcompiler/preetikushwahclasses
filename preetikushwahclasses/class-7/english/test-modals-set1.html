Section A — Fill in with the Correct Modal [5 × 1 = 5]
Q1.1
You _______ wash your hands before eating. (should / would)
Q2.1
_______ I borrow your pencil, please? (May / Must)
Q3.1
She _______ swim very well. (can / may)
Q4.1
We _______ obey the traffic rules. (must / would)
Q5.1
It _______ rain today. Take an umbrella. (might / can)
Section B — Identify the Use of Modals [5 × 2 = 10]
Q6.2
Identify what the modal expresses (ability, permission, obligation, possibility, request):
(a) Can you play the harmonium?
(b) You must complete your homework on time.
Q7.2
Identify the use:
(a) May I come in, sir?
(b) It could rain in the evening.
Q8.2
Identify the use:
(a) Would you pass me the salt?
(b) She should eat healthy food.
Q9.2
Identify the use:
(a) I shall return your book tomorrow.
(b) You ought to respect your teachers.
Q10.2
Identify the use:
(a) He could run faster when he was younger.
(b) Students must wear their school uniform.

Answer Key & Detailed Solutions
Q1. [1 Mark]
should — Expresses advice/duty. Washing hands is a recommended action.
Q2. [1 Mark]
May — Expresses polite permission/request. “May I” is used to ask permission politely.
Q3. [1 Mark]
can — Expresses ability. She has the ability to swim.
Q4. [1 Mark]
must — Expresses obligation/compulsion. Traffic rules are mandatory.
Q5. [1 Mark]
might — Expresses possibility (less certain). Rain is possible but not certain.
Q6. [2 Marks]
(a) Ability — “Can” asks about someone’s ability to play. (1 mark)
(b) Obligation — “Must” expresses a strong duty that cannot be ignored. (1 mark)
Q7. [2 Marks]
(a) Permission — “May I” is a polite way to ask permission. (1 mark)
(b) Possibility — “Could” shows a less certain possibility. (1 mark)
Q8. [2 Marks]
(a) Request — “Would you” is a polite request. (1 mark)
(b) Advice — “Should” gives a suggestion or recommendation. (1 mark)
Q9. [2 Marks]
(a) Promise/Future intention — “Shall” with “I” expresses a promise. (1 mark)
(b) Moral obligation/Duty — “Ought to” expresses a moral duty. (1 mark)
Q10. [2 Marks]
(a) Past ability — “Could” shows ability in the past (he could run faster before). (1 mark)
(b) Obligation/Compulsion — “Must” shows it is compulsory. (1 mark)
Q11. [2 Marks]
Could / Would you please help me carry these books? (1 mark)
Reason: Expresses a polite request. Both “could” and “would” are polite. (1 mark)
Q12. [2 Marks]
Patients must not smoke in the hospital. (1 mark)
Reason: Expresses prohibition/strict rule. Smoking is strictly forbidden. (1 mark)
Q13. [2 Marks]
When I was five, I could not ride a bicycle. (1 mark)
Reason: Expresses past ability (or lack of it). “Could” is the past form of “can”. (1 mark)
Q14. [2 Marks]
You should see a doctor if the fever continues. (1 mark)
Reason: Expresses advice/suggestion. “Should” is used for recommendations. (1 mark)
Q15. [2 Marks]
We should / must plant more trees to save the environment. (1 mark)
Reason: Expresses advice/duty. Planting trees is a moral responsibility. (1 mark)
