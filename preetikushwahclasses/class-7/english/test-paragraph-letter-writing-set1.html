Model Answers
Q1.
A paragraph is a group of related sentences about a single topic. A good paragraph usually has 5 to 8 sentences — a topic sentence, supporting sentences, and a concluding sentence.
Q2.
A topic sentence is the first sentence of a paragraph that introduces the main idea. All other sentences in the paragraph support this idea.
Q3.
The two main types of letters are: (1) Formal letters (official, to authorities/strangers) and (2) Informal letters (personal, to friends/family).
Q4.
A formal letter begins with “Dear Sir/Madam,” or “Respected Sir/Madam,” (if the name is not known) or “Dear Mr./Mrs. [Name],” (if known).
Q5.
The sender’s address is written at the top left corner of the letter, followed by the date.
Q6.
Model paragraph: My best friend is Ananya, who studies with me in Class 7. She is kind, helpful, and always makes me laugh. We sit together in class and share our lunch every day. After school, we often play badminton and do homework together. She helps me whenever I find any subject difficult. I feel very lucky to have such a wonderful friend in my life.
Q7.
Model paragraph: My favourite festival is Diwali, the festival of lights. It is celebrated in October or November every year. My family cleans and decorates the entire house with diyas and rangoli. We wear new clothes and share sweets with our neighbours. In the evening, we perform Lakshmi Puja together. I love Diwali because it brings joy, togetherness, and happiness to everyone.
Q8.
Model paragraph: Trees are very important for all living beings on Earth. They give us oxygen to breathe and provide shade from the hot sun. Many trees give us delicious fruits, nuts, and medicines. Trees also help in preventing soil erosion and reducing air pollution. Sadly, many trees are being cut down for buildings and roads. We must plant more trees and protect our forests to keep our planet green and healthy.
Q9.
Correct order: (b), (d), (a), (c), (e)
“Arjun wants to become a famous cricketer. He is a student of Class 7 in Sunrise Public School. He practises every day after school. His coach says he has great talent. Arjun dreams of playing for India one day.”
Q10.
Format of a Formal Letter:
1. Sender’s Address (top left)
2. Date
3. Receiver’s Address
4. Subject
5. Salutation (Dear Sir/Madam,)
6. Body (3 paragraphs: introduction, main content, conclusion)
7. Complimentary Close (Yours faithfully / Yours sincerely)
8. Signature and Name
