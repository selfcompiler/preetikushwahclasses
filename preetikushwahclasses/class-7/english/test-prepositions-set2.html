Answer Key & Solutions
Q1. [1 Mark]
(b) due to — ‘Due to’ is a compound preposition meaning ‘because of’.
Q2. [1 Mark]
(b) She is married to Rohan. — The correct preposition with ‘married’ is ‘to’, not ‘with’.
Q3. [1 Mark]
(c) both are correct — ‘From Delhi to Mumbai’ and ‘between Delhi and Mumbai’ are both valid.
Q4. [1 Mark]
(a) since — ‘Since’ is used with a point in time (2015). ‘For’ is used with a duration.
Q5. [1 Mark]
Besides — It means ‘in addition to’ and acts as a preposition here.
Q6. [2 Marks]
Since = used with a point in time: I have lived here since 2020. [1 mark]
For = used with a period/duration: I have lived here for five years. [1 mark]
Q7. [2 Marks]
(a) She complained to the manager about him. — ‘Complain to’ (a person) ‘about’ (a thing/person). [1 mark]
(b) I congratulated him on his success. — ‘Congratulate on’ is correct, not ‘for’. [1 mark]
Q8. [2 Marks]
(a) The frog jumped into the pond. (‘Into’ shows movement from outside to inside.) [1 mark]
(b) She is sitting on the sofa. (‘On’ shows position on a surface; no movement.) [1 mark]
Q9. [2 Marks]
(a) The thief climbed over the wall and jumped to the other side. [1 mark]
(b) We walked across the bridge to reach the temple. [1 mark]
Q10. [2 Marks]
beside = next to / by the side of: She sat beside her friend in class. [1 mark]
besides = in addition to: Besides Hindi, she speaks English and Marathi. [1 mark]
Q11. [3 Marks]
(a) The plane flew above the clouds for two hours. — ‘For’ is used with duration, not ‘since’. [1 mark]
(b) She is angry with her brother. — ‘Angry with’ a person is correct, not ‘angry on’. [1 mark]
(c) We reached the station on time. — ‘Reach’ is a transitive verb; no preposition needed before the object. [1 mark]
Q12. [3 Marks]
During — time; to — direction; at — place; near — place; In — time; through — direction/place [1 mark for identifying 4+, 1 mark for 3+ correct types, 1 mark for all correct]
Q13. [3 Marks]
(a) among — used for more than two (all students); preposition of distribution. [1 mark]
(b) between — used for two people (Ravi and Sanjay). [1 mark]
(c) inside / in — preposition of place (enclosed space). [1 mark]
Q14. [3 Marks]
(a) in front of: The garden is in front of our house. [1 mark]
(b) on behalf of: The class monitor spoke on behalf of all the students. [1 mark]
(c) in spite of: In spite of the heavy rain, the match continued. [1 mark]
Q15. [3 Marks]
Sample: My school is in the centre of the city. There is a big playground behind the main building. The library is on the first floor. We sit under the peepal tree during lunch break.
[1 mark per 2 correct prepositions used, max 3 marks]
