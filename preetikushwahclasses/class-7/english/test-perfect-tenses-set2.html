Answer Key & Detailed Solutions
Q1. [1 Mark]
(b) had written — Past perfect tense. The writing happened before the postman came (two past actions, the earlier one takes “had”).
Q2. [1 Mark]
(b) have lived — Present perfect tense. “For ten years now” indicates an action starting in the past and continuing to the present.
Q3. [1 Mark]
(c) will have finished — Future perfect tense. “By the time you reach” shows completion before a future point.
Q4. [1 Mark]
(c) already — Signal words for present perfect: already, just, yet, ever, never, so far, recently, since, for.
Q5. [1 Mark]
(c) gone — go (V1) → went (V2) → gone (V3/past participle).
Q6. [2 Marks]
(a) The doctor had examined the patient before the ambulance arrived. (1 mark)
(b) Past Perfect Tense — used because two past actions occurred; the earlier one (examining) takes past perfect. (1 mark)
Q7. [2 Marks]
(a) I have never eaten Japanese food. (1 mark)
(b) It tells us the speaker has no experience of eating Japanese food from birth until now. Present perfect is used for life experiences. (1 mark)
Q8. [2 Marks]
(a) By December, the school will have organised three cultural events. (1 mark)
(b) Structure: Subject + will have + past participle (V3) (1 mark)
Q9. [2 Marks]
(a) “since” — since childhood (“since” is used with a point in time). (1 mark)
(b) “for” — for two hours (“for” is used with a period/duration of time). (1 mark)
Q10. [2 Marks]
(a) She has completed her painting. — “She” (3rd person singular) takes “has”, not “have”. (1 mark)
(b) They had gone to the market before it rained. — Past participle of “go” is “gone”, not “went”. (1 mark)
Q11. [3 Marks]
Present Perfect: Uses has/have + V3. Connects past action to the present. Example: “I have finished my homework.” (The homework is done now.) (1 mark)
Past Perfect: Uses had + V3. Shows an action completed before another past action. Example: “I had finished my homework before dinner.” (Finished first, then dinner happened.) (1 mark)
Rule: “Has” with he/she/it; “have” with I/we/you/they (present perfect). “Had” with all subjects (past perfect). (1 mark)
Q12. [3 Marks]
has scored — Present Perfect Tense (1 mark)
had promised — Past Perfect Tense (1 mark)
will have bought — Future Perfect Tense (1 mark)
Q13. [3 Marks]
Present Perfect: Sunita has written a letter to her friend. (1 mark)
Past Perfect: Sunita had written a letter to her friend. (1 mark)
Future Perfect: Sunita will have written a letter to her friend. (1 mark)
Q14. [3 Marks]
(a) break → broken (½ mark)
(b) choose → chosen (½ mark)
(c) drink → drunk (½ mark)
(d) fly → flown (½ mark)
(e) forget → forgotten (½ mark)
(f) swim → swum (½ mark)
Q15. [3 Marks]
Sample paragraph:
“Our class has planned a picnic to Pachmarhi next month. (Present Perfect) Last year, we had visited Sanchi Stupa and everyone enjoyed it. (Past Perfect) The teachers had already booked the bus before they informed us. (Past Perfect) By the end of this trip, we will have explored two historical sites. (Future Perfect) I have never been so excited before!” (Present Perfect)
(1 mark per correct tense used)
