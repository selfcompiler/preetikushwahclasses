✏ State Capitals Quiz — 20 Questions

Click an option to check instantly. Covers all regions!
Q1
What is the capital of Rajasthan?
✅ Jaipur is the capital of Rajasthan. It is called the "Pink City" — famous for Hawa Mahal and Amer Fort.
Q2
The capital of Uttar Pradesh is ___
✅ Lucknow is the capital of Uttar Pradesh — known as the "City of Nawabs".
Q3
Which city is the capital of Maharashtra?
✅ Mumbai is the capital of Maharashtra — it is also India's financial capital.
Q4
The capital of West Bengal is ___
✅ Kolkata is the capital of West Bengal — previously called Calcutta, it was the capital of British India.
Q5
What is the capital of Tamil Nadu?
✅ Chennai (formerly Madras) is the capital of Tamil Nadu — major port city and cultural hub of South India.
Q6
The capital of Madhya Pradesh is ___
✅ Bhopal is the capital of Madhya Pradesh — known as the "City of Lakes".
Q7
What is the capital of Gujarat?
✅ Gandhinagar is the capital of Gujarat — named after Mahatma Gandhi. Ahmedabad is the largest city but NOT the capital.
Q8
The capital of Karnataka is ___
✅ Bengaluru (Bangalore) is the capital of Karnataka — known as the "Silicon Valley of India".
Q9
The capital of Assam is ___
✅ Dispur is the capital of Assam (it is a locality within Guwahati). Guwahati is the largest city but Dispur is the official capital.
Q10
What is the capital of Himachal Pradesh?
✅ Shimla is the capital of Himachal Pradesh — it was the summer capital of British India.
Q11
The capital of Odisha is ___
✅ Bhubaneswar is the capital of Odisha — known as the "Temple City of India". Cuttack was the old capital.
Q12
What is the capital of Kerala?
✅ Thiruvananthapuram (formerly Trivandrum) is the capital of Kerala.
Q13
The capital of Arunachal Pradesh is ___
✅ Itanagar is the capital of Arunachal Pradesh — the "Land of the Rising Sun".
Q14
Which city is the capital of Uttarakhand?
✅ Dehradun is the capital of Uttarakhand. It is located in the Doon Valley at the foothills of the Himalayas.
Q15
The capital of Mizoram is ___
✅ Aizawl is the capital of Mizoram — one of the Seven Sister States of Northeast India.
Q16
What is the capital of Goa?
✅ Panaji (also spelled Panjim) is the capital of Goa — India's smallest state by area.
Q17
The capital of Jharkhand is ___
✅ Ranchi is the capital of Jharkhand — known as the "City of Waterfalls".
Q18
What is the capital of Sikkim?
✅ Gangtok is the capital of Sikkim — India's least populous state, known for Kangchenjunga (3rd highest peak).
Q19
The capital of Telangana is ___
✅ Hyderabad is the capital of Telangana. Telangana was carved from Andhra Pradesh in 2014 — India's newest state at that time.
Q20
The capital of Andhra Pradesh (post-2014) is ___
✅ Amaravati is the designated capital of Andhra Pradesh (after Hyderabad became the capital of newly formed Telangana in 2014).

📌 Important to Remember

  • J&K and Ladakh became separate UTs on 31 October 2019 (were previously one state)
  • Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu merged into one UT in 2020
  • Chandigarh is a UT but serves as capital of both Punjab and Haryana states
  • Puducherry has its own elected legislature (like Delhi and J&K)
  • The Andaman & Nicobar Islands are located in the Bay of Bengal; Lakshadweep is in the Arabian Sea
